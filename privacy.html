Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2025

Writers Fund Inc ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website (writersfund.vc) or apply for funding.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, such as when you submit an application form. This may include your name, email address, and details about your writing projects. We use Typeform to process these applications.

We also automatically collect certain non-personal information when you visit our site, such as your IP address, browser type, and usage data, through Google Analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and review your application for funding.
  • Communicate with you regarding our services.
  • Improve our website and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers (like Typeform and Google Analytics) who assist us in operating our website and conducting our business.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your information. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: legal@writersfund.vc
